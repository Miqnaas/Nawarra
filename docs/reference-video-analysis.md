## Repeated visual cues carried into the experience

- Near-black navy setting, low atmospheric detail and a centered 3D subject.
- Cool cyan pathways for anatomy and circuit flow; warm gold for charge and energy transfer.
- Fine callout rules, compact uppercase scientific labels and a slim cyan title accent.
- The visual rhythm of wide torso → close device → separated materials → mechanism diagram → integrated torso.
- A schematic waveform is retained as a teaching aid, explicitly labeled as not measured data.

## Reconstruction choices and limitations

No source mesh or validated TENG dimensions were provided in the reference clip. The heart, torso, vascular routes, electrodes, support films, fiber network and circuit components are therefore procedural Three.js geometry. They are interactive and three-dimensional, but are not substitutes for validated anatomical models, measured material data, a circuit design or a medical device. Charge polarity and movement are explanatory illustrations of contact/separation behavior; the simulation does not claim an output magnitude or clinical feasibility.
